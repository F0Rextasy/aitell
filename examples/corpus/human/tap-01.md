# tap-01.md — human sample (label: human)

The tap in the spare bathroom dripped all winter. Not a steady stream, just a tick every few seconds, loud enough to hear from the hallway at night. I finally shut the valves under the sink on a Saturday morning and took the handle apart with nothing but an adjustable wrench and a butter knife I no longer cared about. The washer inside had gone hard and cracked. A pack of replacements cost less than a sandwich at the corner shop. Twenty minutes later the drip was gone. Silence is underrated.
