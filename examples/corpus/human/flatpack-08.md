# flatpack-08.md — human sample (label: human)

The shelf took four hours. The instructions showed a cheerful person tightening a cam lock with two fingers, which I now believe is a fiction invented to sell furniture. Step nine required three hands and a vocabulary I am glad the neighbors did not hear. One dowel split. I glued it and pretended that was the plan all along. It stands. It holds books. When friends admire it I say thanks and change the subject fast, because the back panel is on upside down and I refuse to revisit step four.
