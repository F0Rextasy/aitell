# market-05.md — human sample (label: human)

Go early. The Saturday market rewards the first hour and punishes everyone else with wilted greens and parking fines. I bring cash because the card reader at the mushroom stall has never worked, not once in four years, and I have stopped asking. The honey man remembers my name but not my face, which leads to a warm greeting followed by visible confusion. Last week I bought too many plums. No regrets. Plum jam forgives everything, including my tendency to double every recipe and hope.
