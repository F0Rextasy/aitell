# dog-04.md — human sample (label: human)

Biscuit refuses to walk past the blue house on our street. No one knows why. He will plant all four feet on the pavement and stare at its gate like it owes him money, and no treat has ever changed his mind, though the cheese cubes came close once. Our walks now take the long way around the block. It adds six minutes. I have started to enjoy the longer route because the far corner has a plum tree that overhangs the fence in August. Biscuit gets a detour and I get plums. Fair trade.
