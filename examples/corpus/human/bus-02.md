# bus-02.md — human sample (label: human)

The 47 changed its route in March without much warning. My stop vanished from the timetable taped inside the shelter, and for a week I kept arriving at the old corner out of habit before remembering. The new stop sits two blocks further east past the laundromat with the flickering sign. I complained to anyone who would listen. Then I noticed the new route passes the bakery, and now I get off one stop early on Fridays. Some detours pay in rye bread.
