# rain-06.md — human sample (label: human)

It rained for nine days straight in October and the river path turned to soup. I walked it anyway on the fifth day because the house felt smaller with every hour indoors, and came home with one soaked shoe and a mood that did not lift until Thursday. The sun returned on a Tuesday while I was stuck in a meeting, which felt personal. By the weekend the path had drained and the air smelled like wet bark and cold. I walked it twice. Some weeks you take the win where you find it.
