# soup-03.md — human sample (label: human)

My grandmother never measured anything. A handful of barley went in when the pot looked thin, and salt went in until she nodded, which took years to learn to read. I wrote down her soup recipe three times and got three different soups. The trick, as far as I can tell, is browning the onions past the point where a careful cook would stop. Nearly burnt. That is where the depth comes from. Mine still tastes like an approximation, but it is a good approximation, and the kitchen smells right for hours.
