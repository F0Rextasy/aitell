# letter-07.md — human sample (label: human)

I found a letter my father wrote in 1987 while cleaning the attic last spring. Two pages about a car that would not start and a neighbor who fixed it for the price of a beer. Nothing important happened in it. That is what got me. He described the frost on the windshield for half a page like it mattered, and maybe it did, because I sat on a dusty box and read the whole thing twice. I put it back in the envelope. Some papers you keep exactly where they were.
